# India's products, what changed today.

Every day, some Indian products are added or edited on Open Food Facts. This page shows the day's counts: how many products were added, how many were changed, and which kinds of change were most common (for example nutrition figures or ingredients). To make it concrete, it also shows one real example from the day: the product's name and brand, what changed, and a link to the product on OFF.

## What this page can and cannot tell you

- "Today" means the most recent file OFF has published. It runs from about 06:30 UTC the day before to 06:30 UTC today.
- The counts and the example are a record of edits. It does not say whether an edit was right.
- Some days hundreds of products arrive in one burst, probably a bulk import. The page shows these as they are and does not judge them.
- We do not show who made an edit.
