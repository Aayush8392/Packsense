# How we differ from Open Food Facts

We do not replace Open Food Facts (OFF). We depend on it. OFF is where the products are written and fixed. We are a second pair of eyes that keeps a record and reports what it sees.

**What OFF is good at.** OFF has millions of products, thousands of volunteers, and photos of the packs. It already checks its own data: its software flags a product when, for example, the energy figure does not fit the nutrients. In a sample of 81,951 recently changed products from 13 days in October 2026, about 31% carried at least one OFF nutrition flag. We did not build a rival to that.

**What we add.** The table below is the short version.

| Question | What OFF gives you | What PackSense adds |
|---|---|---|
| How did this product look last week? | The latest version | Every daily version we saw, kept |
| How much is edited each day? | Not summarised | Daily counts of edits by kind (name, nutrition, ingredients and so on) |
| Is the data improving? | Not summarised | Open flags per day, grouped by kind |
| Which brands fill in their products well? | You can browse a brand | A ranking of brands with 30 or more products, by how many key fields are filled |
| Was a missing product deleted or moved? | The daily files carry no deletion marker | We ask OFF about each missing product and record the answer: gone, moved to another OFF database, or still there |
| What changed in India today? | You can filter, but there is no daily list | A daily summary for India, with a real example |
| Does the arithmetic hold? | OFF's own checks | Our own 11 checks, run on each source of nutrition data separately |
| Where do two sources of nutrition data disagree? | No such check, as of October 2026 | A detector for this |

**What we do not claim.** We cannot tell you whether a number on a pack is true. We tell you whether OFF's entry agrees with itself and with OFF's own rules. We do not edit OFF. Any fix we ever suggest is for a person to review and make.

**Our copy is not OFF.** For India we hold almost every product OFF had in mid-October, plus everything edited since. For other countries we hold only the products edited since 30 September 2026. A product missing here may still be on OFF.
