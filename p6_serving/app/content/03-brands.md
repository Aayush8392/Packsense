# Which brands fill in their product information?

When a brand's products are listed on Open Food Facts, each one should say what is in it, how much, and how healthy it is rated. Many do not. This page ranks brands by how much of that information is filled in.

For each product, seven things are checked: nutrition figures, Nutri-Score, NOVA group (how processed the food is), pack size, ingredients, categories and labels (such as organic or vegetarian). A brand's score is the share of those seven that are filled in across all its products.

## What this page can and cannot tell you

- It shows how complete the entries are. It does not show whether the entries are correct, and it says nothing about the quality of the food.
- Only brands with 30 or more products are ranked. Smaller brands would give noisy results.
- Only the products PackSense holds are counted. A brand may sell more than is shown.
- Brand names are typed by volunteers, so "Nestle" and "Nestlé" can appear as two brands. Brands are grouped on the first brand listed, in lower case, and nothing smarter yet.
- The page does not publish how many errors each brand has. This page is about completeness only.
