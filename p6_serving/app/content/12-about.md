# Where this comes from, and what not to rely on it for.

**Where the data comes from.** Open Food Facts, a free database of packaged food made by volunteers. This site contains data from Open Food Facts, available under the Open Database License. PackSense is an independent project. Open Food Facts does not run it or vouch for it.

**What is held.** All of India, as of mid-October 2026, plus everything edited since. For other countries, only products edited since 30 September 2026.

**How fresh it is.** The copy is made every morning. Every page shows the time it was built. A product can be up to a day behind its page on Open Food Facts.

**What not to use it for.** Do not use this site to decide whether a food is safe for you, for example if you have an allergy. The data is edited by volunteers and can be wrong or out of date. Check the pack.

**How things are worded.** The site says "flagged" and "consistent". It never says "correct", because nobody holds the true figures.

**People's names.** Open Food Facts publishes the usernames of its volunteers. They are not republished. They are removed from what the site shows, and how well that works is measured. It is a measured limit, not a promise of perfection.
