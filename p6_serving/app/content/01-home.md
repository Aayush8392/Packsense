# Packaged food data, checked every day.

Open Food Facts is a free database of packaged food, written by volunteers all over the world. Anyone can add a product or fix one. That makes it large and useful. It also means mistakes get in: a calorie figure that does not match the fat and sugar listed beside it, a missing ingredient list, a product entered twice.

PackSense takes a copy of that database every morning and keeps every version of every product it sees. It then checks the copy and shows you what it finds. It starts with India.

## What you can do on this site

- See which brands fill in their product information completely.
- See whether the data is getting better or worse over time.
- See what changed in India today.
- Download the India data for free.
- See how raw data becomes the tables on this site, step by step, using real products.

**What PackSense does not do.** It never says a product is right or wrong, because nobody holds the true figures. It says whether an entry agrees with itself and with Open Food Facts's own rules. The words used are "flagged" and "consistent".
